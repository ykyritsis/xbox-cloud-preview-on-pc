# 0.21.0 — Library and game-page polish

- Home now keeps news on each Steam game's detail page. Its shelves focus on collections, Steam popularity, personal Steam playtime, and random installed games.
- The Home backdrop has a subtle vignette. Library collage tiles have spacing between their four covers.
- Game pages use a darker left-to-right image fade and clearer Play, provider, and Game options actions.
- Steam's launcher asks whether to open the desktop library or Big Picture mode.
- Choose artwork can check Xbox Store, PlayStation Store, and IGN alongside SteamGridDB and official Steam assets. These catalog matches are best-effort and may not exist for every title.
- Game Options can move any non-launcher title between Games and Apps. The choice is saved locally and Apps do not appear on Home.
- The prior settings and installed-launcher improvements are included in this portable build.

The app is an independent launcher, not a Microsoft or Xbox product. Artwork and metadata remain subject to their source providers' availability and terms.
